Common Questions

Answers Before You Call

How fast can you deliver equipment in Lakeland and Winter Haven?
Most orders placed before 2pm are delivered the same day anywhere in Lakeland, Winter Haven and the greater Polk County area. We also run 24/7 emergency dispatch for breakdowns and urgent project starts — call (863) 555-0100 and you'll reach a live person, not a voicemail.
Do you offer operated equipment rentals in Polk County?
Yes. We supply certified, insured operators for excavators, dozers, cranes and aerial lifts on daily, weekly or monthly terms throughout Lakeland, Winter Haven, Bartow, Haines City and surrounding communities.
What is the minimum rental period?
One day. Weekly and monthly rates offer significant savings — typically 20–35% versus daily pricing. If your timeline shifts, you can extend on the same contract with a single phone call.
Are your machines insured and maintained?
Every unit is fully insured, DOT-compliant for transport, and serviced on a documented preventive maintenance schedule before it leaves our Lakeland yard. Inspection records are available on request.
Do you require a deposit?
Most rentals require a valid ID, proof of insurance and a refundable security deposit. Established commercial accounts in Florida can qualify for net-30 terms with no deposit — ask your account manager during quoting.
Can you deliver to a job site without a loading dock?
Absolutely. Our transport fleet includes roll-back and lowboy trailers, and our drivers handle off-road, tight urban and restricted-access sites across Polk County routinely. Send us site photos with your request and we'll plan the delivery in advance.
Do you serve all of Polk County, Florida?
Yes. We deliver to Lakeland, Winter Haven, Bartow, Haines City, Auburndale, Davenport, Lake Wales, Mulberry, Polk City, Dundee, Frostproof, Fort Meade and surrounding areas. Delivery is included within our core 50-mile service radius.
What brands of equipment do you rent?
Our fleet includes late-model units from Caterpillar, John Deere, Komatsu, Bobcat, Genie, JLG, Skyjack and Takeuchi, all maintained on a documented preventive schedule.
